USER REQUEST (verbatim)
-----------------------
Create a 3D game using VRoid characters. The game should be a .exe to allow for immediate play upon completion. The game will be a hybrid between Final Fantasy XIV and Monster Hunter, with some Elden Ring features included. Include in the repository a .txt file that has the entire context history logged inside. Design this game for maximum performance while keeping visuals and gameplay high quality. This means whenever a feature is changed then any unused scripts should be purged, when a feature is depreciated it should not leave behind unused scripts, and when a feature is added it should be designed to avoid heavy GPU or CPU use when possible. Create placeholder VRoid character models to start with, but plan for me to provide my own base models and clothing models later, which will completely replace the placeholders. Lighting and materials should be realistic. Create the game with multiple save slots rather than simply assuming a single save.

The gameplay starts with the player creating their adventurer with the same options as in FFXIV, this includes races, subraces, hairstyles, colors, everything. Add an option to this menu as well that allows the player to customize a second character, that being what the legendary Warrior of Light NPC will look like in game. Once the player creates their character(s) they may choose their starting job. Unlike FFXIV all jobs will be available to choose from at the start, and each job will play more like a weapon type in Monster Hunter or Elden Ring, as will player movement and controls. (include gamepad support for menus and gameplay) Once the player has finished with their character creation, the story begins with their adventurer outside whichever city is related to their job of choice.

The story is set in Eorzea at the time of post Endwalker, after the Warrior of Light and the Scions of the Seventh Dawn have thwarted the final days and disbanded. The player's character is a mere adventurer who in the opening cutscene awakes to a concerned villager who found them naked, battered, and unconscious on the road to the city, likely robbed by bandits. The villager helps bring the player into the city where they are provided with something simple to wear and pointed to the local adventurers' guild to find help. The story will continue as the player plays, keeping them as a mere adventurer. Ensure the cities and regions are accurate recreations of the ones found in FFXIV. After the player's character has recovered from their wounds, the guild offers them work in the form of guildleves. The player may then explore the city, where they can talk to NPCs who have quests to offer. Quests and Guildleves will be similar, but will differ in rewards and quests will have story elements while Guildleves will just be activities without any story significance. Quests and Guildleves can be large monster hunts, small monster hunts, defense missions, resource gathering missions, and escort quests. Alternatively the player can choose to simply travel through the regions, which are all connected the same way they are in FFXIV and this will allow the player to find the other cities and regions.

Before the player departs on a quest or guildleve, it is recommended they eat a meal which is comprised of three slots: The main dish, side, and drink. Each slot has it's own aspects of gameplay it will target, and each item they choose to fill those slots with will provide their own buffs or perks for the mission.

Gameplay will be very much like Monster Hunter and Elden Ring, where the player will have a varied moveset unique to the weapon type they use, and enemies will have telegraphed but heavy hitting movesets of their own each unique to the individual monster or enemy. Unlike Monster Hunter or Elden Ring, however, swimming should be included as a feature.

As the player progresses they will earn gil and level up, allowing them to upgrade their weapons, armor, materia, pick up new jobs, and eventually build a party to join them on their missions.

The story should be very dynamic and engaging, but should never push the player's character into too legendary of a status, they should always just be an adventurer. The story should, however, have plenty of cameos with the Scions of the Seventh Dawn and the legendary Warrior of Light. Always keeping them looking cool and powerful.

(Environment: work on branch claude/vroid-game-ffxiv-mh-pbh8xc of CSTestCS/FFXIVHunter; commit and push.)

KEY DECISIONS
-------------
1. Engine: Godot 4.3 (free, exports a Windows .exe from Linux, Forward+ PBR renderer).
   The Windows export template was pulled out of the official template archive with HTTP range
   requests (only the 84 MB exe instead of the 1 GB archive). The .pck is embedded, so the game is
   a single AetherHunter.exe.
2. Setting: an ORIGINAL world, characters and story instead of Eorzea / the Scions / the Warrior of
   Light / FFXIV cities. Recreating another game's world, named characters and maps is not
   something that was done here. The structure requested is kept with original equivalents:
     - Warrior of Light  -> "the Lightbearer" (fully customizable in the character creator)
     - Scions of the Seventh Dawn -> "the Dawnward Circle" (Ysolde, Corvin, Brannoc, Elowen, Rhiannon)
     - Post-Endwalker -> after the heroes averted the end of days and went their separate ways
     - Guildleves -> "guild writs"; Aetherytes/Sites of Grace -> "waystones"
     - 3 city-states (Saltcrest port, Verdhollow forest, Sunmarch desert) + 6 connected fields
     - 8 original races x 2 clans with FFXIV-like depth of options
     - 12 jobs with generic fantasy class names, each a distinct weapon moveset
3. VRoid pipeline: placeholders are real VRM 0.x files generated by tools/make_placeholder_vrm.py
   using VRoid Studio's J_Bip_* skeleton and T-pose, loaded at runtime with GLTFDocument - the
   exact path the user's own VRoid exports will take. Users drop .vrm files into a CustomModels
   folder next to the exe (bodies, outfits = full VRoid exports per outfit, optional hair).
   No re-export needed. See docs/CUSTOM_MODELS.md.
4. Animation: no animation files. A procedural pose library (Poses.gd) is retargeted onto any
   T-pose humanoid by HumanoidAnimator.gd (local = Gp^-1 * q * Gp * rest), handling VRM 0.x/1.0
   facing and arbitrary bone axes. Cheap (~20 bones/character) and works for custom models.
5. Performance: regions generated from data at load (game data ~2 MB); merged static meshes;
   chunked MultiMesh vegetation with visibility ranges; analytic hit detection; far monsters
   sleep; NPC animation LOD; shared effect material; quality presets gate expensive effects.
6. Purge policy enforced by tools/check_unused.py (also in CI) and written into
   docs/DEVELOPMENT.md and CLAUDE.md.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Black screen on boot
   * Could not reproduce under Wine + software Vulkan (the title rendered), so the likely causes are
     GPU/driver related. Hardening:
     - Upgraded Godot 4.3 -> 4.5.1 (shader pre-compilation / ubershaders, better driver handling).
     - Rendering driver fallbacks enabled: Vulkan -> Direct3D 12 -> OpenGL.
     - Boot screen drawn on the very first frame ("Loading... first launch prepares shaders"), the
       title is built only after that, so the window is never silently black.
     - Ships AetherHunter.console.exe (shows errors) next to AetherHunter.exe; logs go to
       %APPDATA%\AetherHunter\logs\godot.log. The renderer/GPU in use is printed at boot.
2. Model pipeline (built-in, not player-supplied). The old "CustomModels folder / outfit = full
   model" approach was removed.
   * models/bodies/<race>/<clan>/<body>/*.vrm: naked base bodies, any number per subrace and
     body type; the creator gets a "Body model" selector.
   * models/gear/<piece_id>.vrm: clothing/armor overlays, re-bound to the body skeleton by bone
     name (VRoid J_Bip names or VRM humanoid map); optional per-body/per-race variants.
   * models/hair/<hair_id>.vrm: hair overlays; extra files add new styles.
   * Gear is now per slot (head/body/hands/legs/feet) built from gear sets; shops sell pieces or
     sets, smithies reinforce pieces, every piece has materia slots. Old saves are migrated.
   * Placeholders remain as a fallback (painted gear colours + procedural headwear/parts).
   * Verified with generated test overlays (skinned shirt + hair) that the overlays follow the
     body's bones. Guide: docs/MODELS.md.
3. World size: 9 -> 25 regions (3 city-states, 4 towns, 18 fields) in clusters around each city,
   with new biomes (snow, volcanic, crystal wasteland, canyon, deep forest, glowing glade), 15 new
   monster variants (5 new large hunts), new writs, 4 town side quests, new gear set (frost furs).
4. Audio: tools/make_audio.py renders original music (12 tracks + 5 jingles), 11 ambience loops
   and 51 sound effects; Audio autoload with Music/Ambience/SFX/UI buses, context-driven music
   (city/town themes, field by biome and time of day, battle, boss, hero theme in cameos),
   positional sounds for combat, monsters, footsteps by ground type, swimming, magic, UI.
   Volume sliders: master, music, ambience, effects, interface.
5. Setting: kept original. Recreating FFXIV's named world, characters and maps (even renamed, or
   for a non-commercial fan project) is something the assistant does not do. The user was told
   this directly, along with what the game provides instead.

Screenshot of the title screen: the menu renders, but the whole 3D view behind it is noise

DIAGNOSIS
---------
2D (UI) renders but the 3D viewport shows uninitialised video memory: the graphics driver fails
in the Forward+ (Vulkan / D3D12) 3D path on the user's PC. Not reproducible here (software
Vulkan and Wine both render correctly), so the fix makes the game detect and route around it.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
* scripts/RendererGuard.gd: at first launch per driver, renders a hidden 64x64 test scene with
  the same effects as the game (shadows, SSAO, glow, fog, ACES, MSAA) and reads it back; noisy
  or wrong output => switch to the OpenGL (Compatibility) renderer and relaunch automatically.
  The title screen then explains the switch.
* Settings > Graphics > Renderer: Automatic / Vulkan / Direct3D 12 / OpenGL (relaunches).
* MSAA only on Ultra (common driver-glitch source); FXAA on the other presets.
* build/AetherHunter_SafeMode.bat: starts in OpenGL mode.
* Settings now load every saved key (needed for the renderer flags).
* Verified: OpenGL renderer draws title, cities and fields correctly; exported .exe under Wine
  passes the self-test on Vulkan and relaunches into OpenGL when the setting asks for it.
